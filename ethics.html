Ethical Use of Artificial Intelligence

Responsible Use in Academic Settings

AI tools can support learning and productivity, but students must use them responsibly. AI should enhance understanding—not replace critical thinking or independent work.

  • Use AI as a study companion, not a replacement for original work.
  • Always credit AI-assisted contributions if required by your institution.
  • Ensure that AI-generated explanations are verified with credible academic sources.

Avoiding Plagiarism and Misuse

Improper use of AI-generated content can lead to academic dishonesty. Students and researchers must ensure that work submitted reflects personal understanding and original thought.

  • Do not submit AI-generated output as your own writing.
  • Use AI drafts as inspiration or guidance, not final submissions.
  • Follow institutional policies on AI usage.

Accuracy and Verification

AI-generated responses may be incomplete, outdated, or incorrect. Fact-checking and critical evaluation are essential when using AI in research or decision-making.

  • Verify statistics, citations, and claims using trusted sources.
  • Use domain experts or textbooks to confirm sensitive or technical information.
  • Do not rely solely on AI for academic or professional decision-making.

Bias and Fairness

AI models may generate biased content due to the data they were trained on. Ethical users must recognize and correct bias when it appears.

  • Be aware that AI may unintentionally reinforce stereotypes.
  • Cross-check outputs for fairness and inclusivity.
  • Avoid using AI to create misleading or discriminatory content.

Recommended Guidelines for AI Use

Users should follow these principles to ensure ethical and responsible use of AI tools:

  • Use AI with transparency and integrity.
  • Verify all generated information before applying it.
  • Maintain authorship and originality of your work.
  • Respect privacy and avoid sharing sensitive personal data.
  • Promote fairness and accuracy when using AI in communication or decision-making.
